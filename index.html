ABOUT US

Where Creativity Finds Its Expression

Mathurapur School of Art is dedicated to nurturing creativity, artistic skill and self-expression through structured art education.

Building Creative Minds Through Art

Our aim is to create a supportive learning environment where students can explore their imagination, develop artistic skills and express their ideas confidently.

Through drawing, painting, craft, sculpture and creative activities, students are encouraged to learn, experiment and grow as artists.

Our Vision

To encourage young talents to discover the joy of art, develop creative confidence and build strong artistic foundations for the future.
