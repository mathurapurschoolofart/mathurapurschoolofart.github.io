Welcome

Where Creativity Takes Shape

Mathurapur School of Art is a creative learning space where students can explore drawing, painting, craft, sculpture and artistic expression in a friendly, guided environment.

About the School

Learning art with confidence, imagination and joy.

Our aim is to help learners discover their own artistic language through regular practice, personal guidance and a supportive studio environment. The school welcomes learners of different ages and experience levels.
